## 🌟 About ParentWallet

**ParentWallet** is a family financial management application designed to make allowance tracking, expense management, and parent–child communication easier.

Parents can monitor their children's recorded expenses and available balances, while children can track their spending and manage the money they receive.

Built with Flutter and Firebase, ParentWallet brings essential family finance tools together in one application with a modern, user-friendly interface.

## 🛠️ Installation Guide

Follow these steps to install ParentWallet on your Android device.

1. Open the [ParentWallet Releases page](https://github.com/musfiqurjahin/ParentWallet/releases/latest).
2. Select the latest available release.
3. Scroll down to the **Assets** section.
4. Download the APK that matches your device's processor architecture.
5. Once the download is complete, open the APK file.
6. If Android displays a security prompt, allow installation from that source in your device settings.
7. Return to the installer and tap **Install**.
8. After installation finishes, open ParentWallet and follow the on-screen instructions.

**Tip:** ARM64 (`arm64-v8a`) is the appropriate choice for most modern Android phones. If you're unsure about your device's architecture, check your device specifications before downloading.
